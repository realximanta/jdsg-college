Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JDSG College App collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information (Name, Email)
  • Profile Details (Role, Class/Department, Phone Number)
  • Profile Picture

2. How We Use It

Your data is used solely to provide a personalized experience within the app, such as displaying your profile and allowing teachers/students to be identified.

3. Data Storage

Images are stored securely on Cloudflare R2. Metadata is stored on Cloudflare Workers KV.
