Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, we collect your basic profile information (Name, Email Address). During signup, we also collect your Role, Phone Number, and Profile Picture. This data is securely stored to provide you with a personalized experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for institutional purposes—to identify you within the app, display your profile, and allow access to college notifications. We do not sell or share your data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Images are securely hosted on Cloudflare R2, while profile metadata is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. Your session is managed via a secure JWT token. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
